How Android Notification Capture Works
BrokeBuddy uses Android's Notification Listener Service to read payment notifications from apps like Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, and CRED. You explicitly grant notification access in Android Settings → Apps → Special Access → Notification Access. We never collect passwords, UPI PINs, OTPs, or unrelated notifications. Only transaction-relevant fields (amount, direction, merchant, timestamp) are extracted and sent to your BrokeNoMore ledger.

Privacy & Security
No Banking Credentials: We never collect or store banking passwords, UPI PINs, OTPs, or payment credentials.
Selective Reading: Only payment-related notifications from supported apps are processed. Personal messages, social media, and other notifications are ignored.
On-Device Parsing: Transaction extraction runs on your Android device. Only structured transaction data (amount, merchant, type) is sent — never full notification text.
Your Data, Your Control: All captured transactions can be reviewed, corrected, or deleted. Demo data is completely isolated and never mixed with real entries.
No AI-Invented Totals: Financial calculations use only recorded transaction data. AI is used for categorization, never for computing balances or totals.
How to Disconnect
  1. Open Android Settings → Apps → Special App Access
  2. Tap Notification Access
  3. Find BrokeBuddy and toggle it OFF
  4. Optionally, uninstall the BrokeBuddy companion app

Once notification access is revoked, no new transactions will be captured. Existing captured data remains in your ledger until you delete it.
