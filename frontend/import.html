Explicit User Consent & Privacy Notice

Milo AutoSync uses Android's official NotificationListenerService to detect completed Google Pay (com.google.android.apps.nfc.plugin.card.gp) payment notifications on your device.

  • Data Accessed: Payment amount, merchant/counterparty description, timestamp, reference ID.
  • Strict Restrictions: Unrelated app notifications, SMS, Banking Passwords, OTPs, and UPI PINs are never accessed or stored.
  • User Control: You can pause, revoke permission, or purge notification records at any time.
